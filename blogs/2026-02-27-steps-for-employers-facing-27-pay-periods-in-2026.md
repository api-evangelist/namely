Many employers are preparing for a unique payroll challenge in 2026: the year includes 27 pay periods instead of the usual 26. Once every 11-or-so years, the stars align for a 27th biweekly pay period. However, this extra pay period can create budgeting, compliance, and administrative headaches if not handled properly.
