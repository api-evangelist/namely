Open enrollment is one of the most meaningful times of the year for both employees and employers. For employers, the decisions you make around benefits offerings, communications, and resources directly shape how supported and valued your people feel. For employees, benefits selections impact their mental, physical, and financial well-being—and often their families’ well-being too.
